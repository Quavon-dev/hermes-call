## Version page

**Promotional Text** [170]

Your own AI agent, a phone call away. End-to-end encrypted calls and chat with the Hermes agent you
host yourself. Try the built-in demo, no server needed.

**Description** [4000]

Hermes Call connects your iPhone to the AI agent you run yourself (Hermes Agent). Call it, text it,
or let it call you, like a real phone call, also on the Lock Screen.

CALLS BOTH WAYS
• Your agent can ring your iPhone through CallKit when something needs you.
• Call it from the app, Siri, a widget, the Action button or Control Center.
• Live captions, interrupt it any time, push to talk.
• Calls keep going when you switch between Wi-Fi and cellular.

CHAT
• Text, photos, files and voice notes, with Markdown answers, result cards and maps.
• Notifications show who wrote, decrypted on your iPhone; reply right from them.
• Share from any app, search your history, several agents in one list.

YOU DECIDE WHAT IT SEES
• Your agent can ask for your location, calendar, reminders, contacts and more, but every
  capability is off until you set it to Ask or Yes, and every request is logged.
• Approve the commands your agent wants to run with Face ID, never by voice.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Audio, messages and files are end-to-end encrypted between your iPhone and your own bridge.
• No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. The developer runs no server that can read your
  content.

ALSO
• An animated "presence" that listens, thinks and speaks with the real voice.
• Live Activity for longer tasks, Apple Watch app, widgets.

WHAT YOU NEED
Hermes Call is a client for your own setup: Hermes Agent, the free hermes-call-bridge next to it and
a small relay you host (setup guide: github.com/Quavon-dev/hermes-call). Without it, tap
"Try a demo" to explore everything offline with a demo agent.

No account, no sign-in and no QR code are needed: the app has a built-in demo agent that runs offline on the device.

FASTEST WAY IN (no QR code)
1. Launch the app and tap Continue on the intro pages (permission prompts may be skipped).
2. On the last page tap "Try a demo". A demo agent named Atlas is added.
3. On the consent screen tap "Allow sharing with my agent" (or "Not now"; the demo stays on the device either way).

DEMO QR CODE (if you prefer the scanner)
QR image: https://github.com/Quavon-dev/hermes-call/blob/main/ios/appstore/demo-qr.png?raw=true
It encodes: hermescall://demo
Scan it in the app (last intro page > "Add your relay" > "Scan QR") or with the iPhone Camera app; either starts the same demo agent.

WHAT TO TEST
- Chat tab: send "plan my day" (formatted answer), "places for dinner" (place cards with a map) or "help". "/stop" cancels a reply.
- Call tab: tap "Call Atlas". A simulated call with captions starts; no microphone audio is recorded or sent in the demo. Mute, Speaker and hang-up work.
- Settings (gear): agent list (Atlas marked Demo, "Remove demo agent"), Privacy > "Share with my agent", Phone access (all capabilities default to No), Diagnostics.

WHY A QR CODE EXISTS AT ALL
Real use pairs the app with the user's own self-hosted relay and AI agent; the QR code shown by that server is a one-time pairing code. We run no server and there are no accounts, so the demo agent above stands in for it.
